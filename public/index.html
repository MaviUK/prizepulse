How we decide “best value”

Where prize amount and maximum ticket allocation are known, we calculate an illustrative prize-value-to-ticket-cost ratio at full allocation. We exclude variable-prize and instant-win mechanics from simple comparisons.

THE MATH, EXPLAINED

Transparent by design.

PrizePulse is an independent information tool. We do not operate draws or sell entries.

01

Actual price changes

We record published ticket prices at each successful check. A tracked drop requires two recorded prices. A crossed-out “previously” price shown by a promoter is labelled separately as an advertised discount.

02

Full-allocation comparison

Illustrative gross value per ticket = published cash prize or cash alternative ÷ maximum eligible tickets. We compare that amount with one entry's advertised price. This is not a prediction of final results or a guaranteed return.

03

What we don't rank

Instant wins, multiple-winner draws, variable prize pools, or draws without a verified maximum entry count are marked “Insufficient data”. Bulk discounts, cashback, fees, free-entry rules and changing ticket sales can alter real outcomes.

04

Watchlist and alerts

Your watchlist is saved on this device. It highlights a lower price the next time you open or refresh the app. Background push notifications and email alerts are not included in this first version.

Source and limits

Prices and ticket counts are public information from thatprizeguy.co.uk. A source layout change or access restriction can stop monitoring. Always confirm the current terms and availability on the promoter's website. Participation is for adults aged 18+ only.
